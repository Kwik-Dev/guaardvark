SCENE 1 — THE QUIET FOREST

EXT. FOREST PATH - DAY

The forest is unnervingly quiet. No birdsong, no rustle of creatures. Ancient trees stand like silent witnesses on either side of an overgrown trail.

{{Quiet Forest:environment}}
{{Forest Path:environment}}

[[Elara]] (19, with determined eyes and practical, worn-out clothing) walks cautiously along the path. She carries a worn leather {{Satchel:prop}} slung across her shoulder.

SHOT 1 - WIDE: The forest swallows the path. Elara is small against the towering trees. Sunlight filters down in pale shafts.

SHOT 2 - MEDIUM: Elara's hand rests near the {{Knife:prop}} at her belt. Her jaw is set, eyes scanning the shadows.

SFX: Crunch of footsteps on dry leaves, a distant, eerie caw.

Suddenly, a low growl rumbles from the shadows of the trees. A {{Corrupted Wolf:prop}}, its form made of twisted bark and shadow, steps onto the path, blocking her way.

Elara freezes, her hand instinctively going to her knife.

ELARA
(whispering)
Oh no.

The Corrupted Wolf snarls, baring jagged, wooden teeth. Branches crack and splinter as it shifts its weight.

SHOT 3 - CLOSE-UP: Elara's eyes widen, breath caught. The reflected glow of the Lumin Seed pulses faintly from inside her satchel.

MUSIC: Tension_Builds.mp3 - fades in, low strings.

SHOT 4 - LOW ANGLE: The Corrupted Wolf looms over Elara, bark and shadow bleeding together. A primal, ancient threat.

ELARA
(softly, to herself)
Please... just let me pass.

The wolf growls louder, crouching to spring.

MUSIC: Tension_Sting.mp3 - climax hit.

SHOT 5 - WIDE: Elara stands frozen on the path, the wolf between her and the distant Sunstone Spire visible through the trees.

FADE OUT.

SCENE 2 — THE LAST SPARK (OPTIONAL BRANCH PRELUDE)

INT. OLD CABIN - DAY

A dusty sunbeam cuts through the broken window of a small, abandoned cabin. Dust motes dance in the light.

{{Old Cabin:environment}}

On the wooden table, a single, faintly glowing seed pulses with a soft, warm light. This is the {{Lumin Seed:prop}}.

[[Elara]] carefully wraps the seed in a cloth and places it in her {{Satchel:prop}}.

SFX: Rustling cloth, gentle wind through the cracked window.

ELARA
(V.O.)
The Lumin Seed was the last one. The last spark of the Great Tree's light. If I couldn't get it to the Sunstone Spire before the new moon...

She doesn't finish the thought. She shoulders her satchel and steps outside.

SHOT 6 - INSERT: The Lumin Seed wrapped in cloth, its glow dimming for a heartbeat, then steadying.

SHOT 7 - WIDE: Elara exits the cabin, the door creaking shut behind her. The quiet forest waits.

FADE OUT.
